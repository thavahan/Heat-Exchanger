Theory & method

Aim

Conduct heat-transfer tests in parallel- and counter-flow arrangements; determine heat-transfer rate, overall heat-transfer coefficient, and effectiveness.

Apparatus

  • Concentric-tube heat exchanger
  • Measuring flasks
  • Stopwatch and temperature indicators

Core equations

Mass flow rates (1 litre collected)ṁh = 1/th,   ṁc = 1/tc   kg/s
Hot-side heat transferQh = ṁh cp (T1 − T2)
Cold-side heat transferQc = ṁc cp (t2 − t1)
Log mean temperature differenceΔTlm = (ΔT1 − ΔT2) / ln(ΔT1/ΔT2)
Overall coefficientU = Qavg / (A · ΔTlm)
Effectivenessε = Qavg / [Cmin(T1 − t1)]

Procedure

  1. Set valves for the selected flow arrangement.
  2. Switch on the heater and admit cold water.
  3. Adjust hot and cold flow rates to steady values.
  4. Allow temperatures to reach steady state.
  5. Record inlet/outlet temperatures and collection times.
  6. Repeat for several flow rates, then calculate performance.
